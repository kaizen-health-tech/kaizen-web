**NewsCard** — a health article in the Discover feed. Gradient cover (or image), source + date row, title/deck, and read-more with share/bookmark actions.

```jsx
<NewsCard source="Mayo Clinic" date="2d ago" title="Small daily walks lower blood pressure"
  deck="A review of 27 studies suggests 20 minutes a day helps." onOpen={open} onSave={save} />
```
