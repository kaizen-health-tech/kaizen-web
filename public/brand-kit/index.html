Dashboard anatomy

Progressive disclosure

  1. 01
    Orient

    Start with a reassuring, plain-language summary.

  2. 02
    Explain

    Show the few signals that shaped the summary.

  3. 03
    Act

    Offer one clear next step without manufacturing urgency.

Privacy is a product feature.

Make sharing scope, ownership, and reversibility visible at the moment of choice.

04 · Voice & imagery

Warm, direct, never clinical.

We reduce uncertainty without minimizing it. We sound like a knowledgeable guide who respects the person’s agency.

01

Lead with the meaning

“Your results are in the expected range.”

Not “All biomarkers nominal.”

02

Offer a next step

“Bring this summary to your next visit.”

Not “Action may be required.”

03

Respect uncertainty

“This pattern may be worth discussing.”

Not “We found the cause.”

Image direction

Real life, with room to breathe.

Show intergenerational care, quiet confidence, and everyday health moments. Favor natural light and lived-in environments. Avoid staged clinician clichés, fear-based imagery, and glowing futuristic anatomy.
