MEDICAL RECORDS CHECKLIST AND WORKING SUMMARY
Kaizen Health
Guide: https://kaizenhealth.io/blog/family-history/organizing-medical-records

Fill in or print this blank worksheet. Keep the completed copy private.
It does not send information to Kaizen. Do not include account passwords.
Confirm medical instructions with the care team. Mark unknown details as unknown.

GET STARTED
[ ] Ask the person what help they want and who may see which information.
[ ] Gather current medicines, supplements, allergies, and reactions.
[ ] List diagnosed conditions, care contacts, and emergency contacts.
[ ] Collect relevant visit notes, discharge papers, test results, and insurance details.
[ ] Locate applicable advance directives and healthcare-agent documents.
[ ] Choose the main copy, its owner, and a backup helper.
[ ] List missing records and confirm each provider's request process.
[ ] Agree when to check this summary again.
