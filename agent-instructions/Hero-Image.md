Reusable Hero Image Instructions

Create a wide, realistic editorial healthcare photograph for a blog hero image.

Composition

* Use a 16:9 landscape format, ideally 1536 × 864 px or larger.
* Place the main subject on the right third of the frame.
* Preserve generous, uncluttered negative space on the left.
* Treat the image as a cinematic article opener, not an infographic.
* Keep important faces and objects away from the outer edges so the image remains usable across desktop and mobile crops.
* Use natural depth and foreground elements to create dimension.

Subject and storytelling

* Show a believable human moment that communicates the article topic without literally explaining it.
* Use age, setting, props, and activity to establish context.
* Keep the subject engaged in the moment rather than looking at the camera.
* Expressions should feel natural, calm, and understated.
* Avoid exaggerated smiles, staged poses, and generic stock-photo gestures.

Photography style

* Premium documentary-style lifestyle photography
* Natural skin texture, realistic age lines, and authentic proportions
* Soft directional daylight or warm golden-hour light
* Gentle background blur with recognizable environmental context
* Rich but natural shadows
* Controlled highlights without blown-out skin
* Slightly reduced saturation
* Subtle film grain and mild lens softness
* Editorial color grading rather than glossy commercial retouching

Text and graphics

* No generated text
* No headline, subtitle, paragraph, icon row, labels, badges, charts, or decorative typography
* No visible medical claims
* No fabricated app interface unless specifically required by the story
* Let the website handle the title and article copy, a startlingly sensible division of labor

Brand tone

The final image should feel:

* Warm
* Trustworthy
* Human
* Modern
* Health-focused without appearing clinical
* Hopeful without implying guaranteed outcomes
* Premium enough for a contemporary digital-health publication

Avoid

* Hospital brochure aesthetics
* Overly orange lighting
* Artificially smooth skin
* Excessive lens flare
* Perfectly staged environments
* Subjects staring directly into the camera
* Fake medical documents with readable information
* Busy compositions
* Embedded explanatory text
* Watermarks and logos
